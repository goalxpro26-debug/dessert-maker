Dessert Shop

Run your own little dessert shop! Follow each customer's recipe card, step by step, and serve up something sweet.

20 customers · earn coins · buy shop upgrades · no harsh fails — a customer who waits too long just wanders off and you try again.

How to Play

1. Read the recipe card at the top.
2. Tap the kitchen stations in that exact order:
   Bowl → Pour → Pipe → Sprinkle → Fruit → Serve
3. Each correct step adds coins. A wrong tap costs patience.
4. Serve before the patience bar runs out for a tip!
5. Spend coins in the Shop between customers.
6. Serve all 20 customers to win the Golden Whisk!
